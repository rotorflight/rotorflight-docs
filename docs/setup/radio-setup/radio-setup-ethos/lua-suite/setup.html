Setup

Setup covers how the helicopter is built and wired: the board and radio, the sensors, the mixer and servos, and power, motors and the governor. Each section links to the page that explains the settings.

Board & Radio​

Configuration​

The craft name, PID loop speed, and the features the board runs: GPS, LED strip and CMS.

See Configuration.

Ports​

What each UART is used for, and its speed.

Radio Config​

Stick deflection and centre, the throttle range, and the yaw and cyclic deadbands.

See Receiver.

Telemetry​

The telemetry sensors the flight controller sends, grouped by kind. Open a group to turn sensors on or off. Tool selects the default sensors.

See Receiver: Telemetry Sensors.

Controls​

A menu of its own:

PageWhat it is forMore
ModesSwitch ranges for each mode. Pick a mode, then add or change its ranges.Modes
AdjustmentsIn-flight adjustments from a switch or knob.Adjustments
FailsafeWhat each channel does when the signal is lost.Failsafe
BeepersWhich events sound the beeper, and the ESC beacon.Beepers
BlackboxThe logging device, rate and fields, and the log memory's status. Tool on Status erases it.Blackbox
StatsFlight count, and last and total flight time.

Sensors​

Accelerometer​

Accelerometer trim for roll and pitch. Tool calibrates the accelerometer: keep the model level and still while it does.

Alignment​

How the board is mounted, with a live 3D view of the helicopter that follows the board as you move it. Tool turns the view so the tail faces you.

See Configuration.

Mixer & Servos​

Mixer​

The swashplate and tail mixer, in four pages:

PageWhat it is for
SwashSwash type, rotor direction, and the aileron, elevator and collective directions.
GeometryCyclic and collective calibration, geometry correction, and the pitch limits. Tool turns swash setup mode on, to level the swash.
TailTail mode, yaw direction, tail idle and centre offset, and yaw trim and calibration.
TrimsRoll, pitch, collective and yaw trims. Tool turns mixer override on while you set them.

See Mixer and Setup Mixer.

Servos​

PWM Output lists the servos; select one to set its centre, limits and direction. BUS Output is for bus servos and is greyed out unless the model has them. Tool turns servo override on, so a servo moves to its centre as you change it.

See Servos.

Power & Motors​

Power​

PageWhat it is for
BatteryBattery profiles: capacity and cell voltages.
AlertsFlight time alarm and the BEC and receiver voltage alerts.
SourcesWhere voltage and current are measured.
SmartFuelHow fuel (charge left) is worked out.

See Power.

ESC & Motors​

PageWhat it is forMore
Motor OverrideRun the motor from the radio, for testing. Take the blades off and secure the helicopter first.
ThrottleThrottle protocol, update rate and PWM endpoints.Motors
TelemetryESC telemetry protocol and corrections.ESC Telemetry
RPMWhere RPM comes from, the main and tail motor gear ratios and the pole count.RPM Measurement
ESC Prog.Change your ESC's own settings from the radio, through the flight controller. Pick the ESC's make.ESC Forward Programming

Each ESC page shows the ESC it found, then its settings in groups:

Governor​

The governor's mode and timing, in four pages. Its per-profile settings (head speed and gains) are under Flight Tuning → Governor.

PageWhat it is for
GeneralGovernor mode, throttle type, idle and auto throttle, handover throttle and the throttle hold timeout.
Ramp TimeStartup, spoolup, spooldown, tracking and recovery times.
FiltersHead speed and voltage filter cutoffs, TTA and precomp bandwidth, and the D-term cutoff.
Bypass CurveThe throttle curve used when the governor is bypassed.

See Governor.
